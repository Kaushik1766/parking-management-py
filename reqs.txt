Parking management requirements - 

Customer -
1. Signup with email password and office
2. slot allotment on registering vehicles (if any slot is free)
3. reuse slot if slot of required type is assigned to the user
4. add/remove/park/unpark vehicles
5. view parking history (only parkings that are over)
6. view bills if generated
7. get email notifications of generated bills

Admin -
1. Add building
2. Add floors to a building
3. Assign floor of a building to an office (this also creates an office entity)
4. view slots (with availability)
